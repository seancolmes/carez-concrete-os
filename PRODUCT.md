## Platform

web

## Users

Concrete subcontractor operators working in office and field contexts, including estimators, project/operations staff, field crews, and finance users. Nik explicitly requires mobile-friendly and desktop-friendly use.

## Product Purpose

Pourtrace is a concrete subcontractor operating system spanning opportunity and Takeoff through estimate, proposal, project, field production, cost, changes, and billing. It supports work decisions and preserves their lineage.

## Operating Context

Users review PDF plans and Concrete Conditions, measure 2D geometry, prepare estimates and proposals, coordinate project readiness and field work, record production, manage changes, and handle invoicing, payments, and retainage. Office users need dense workstations; construction workers need legible, touch-friendly field workflows.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Seven primary operating surfaces: Today, Preconstruction, Projects, Field, Production, Finance, System.
- Supabase/PostgreSQL is authoritative for company-isolated data and audited workflows.
- Persisted page-coordinate 2D/vector Takeoff geometry is quantity authority; derived 3D is verification.
- Server-authoritative deterministic quantity, cost, pricing, and financial calculations remain distinct from presentation.
- People retain final authority for scope, Conditions, methods, production, pricing, margin, approvals, and billing decisions.
- Existing deep links and record-detail routes remain usable.

## Product Principles

- Bring the next real task and its governing context into view.
- Keep plan quantity, direct cost, and sell separate and traceable.
- Preserve human control over operational and commercial judgment.
- Let desktop and mobile layouts adapt to the work while sharing one product vocabulary.
