# Module Spec — AI Assistance

Status: P7/P8 target with earlier bounded assistance allowed

## Purpose
Reduce setup, recognition, retrieval, comparison, repetition, and QA work while retaining human authority over concrete scope and commercial decisions.

## Good AI uses
Sheet naming/indexing, title-block extraction, scale candidates, document extraction, plan-fact suggestions, revision comparison, search/retrieval, invoice/document matching, repetitive setup, anomaly/QA surfacing.

## Approval tiers
- Automatic, low-risk clerical/indexing tasks.
- Suggest + batch approve for metadata candidates.
- Individual approval for scope-impacting geometry, measurement-role, or Concrete Condition suggestions.
- Advisory only for production rates, pricing, margin, budget, and final commercial decisions.

## Invariants
- Evidence must accompany extracted/suggested facts.
- AI does not silently overwrite authoritative source documents or accepted commercial records.
- Humans remain authoritative for scope, means/methods, production assumptions, pricing, margin, budgets, approvals, and final estimates.
