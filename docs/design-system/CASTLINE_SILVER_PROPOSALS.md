# Castline silver proposals

Status: visual discovery; none approved. These generated images are design studies. Their project names, dates, quantities, prices, logos, and workflow copy are illustrative and do not change product behavior.

All three retain Castline naming, seven-domain top navigation, desktop Condition-first Takeoff, and mobile Field actions. They use silver/graphite gradients and restrained glows without orange or saturated brand accents.

## A — Satin Silver

`castline-silver-a-satin.png` uses a luminous aluminum top bar, pearl workplane, graphite active tab, and quiet silver focus glow. Strongest outdoor readability, but its components need more distinctive detailing to avoid feeling generic.

## B — Titanium

`castline-silver-b-titanium.png` uses a darker metallic gradient and a dark drawing stage. Strongest industrial character, but the dark PDF treatment needs careful testing against real plan legibility and must never alter measurement meaning.

## C — Monochrome Atelier

`castline-silver-c-monochrome.png` uses graphite-to-silver navigation, pale work surfaces, black technical linework, and selective silver glows. Strongest fit for a professional product-wide system; field touch targets and text contrast need validation.

## Recommendation for exploration

Develop C with a light plan canvas and field contrast measured against A. This is a proposal, not an approval or permission to propagate the design.
